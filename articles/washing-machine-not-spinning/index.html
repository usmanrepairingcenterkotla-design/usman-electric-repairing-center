Washing machine not spinning? 6 common causes

Published: 2026-10-20

A machine that washes but won't spin is the most common fault we see. Unplug the machine before you check anything. Here are the six causes we find most often, from the simplest to the most serious.

  1. The drum is overloaded or unbalancedWet clothes bunched on one side stop the spinner from turning. Spread the load evenly, take a few items out and try again.
  2. The spin lid switch is faultyOn twin tub machines, the spinner only runs when the lid switch closes. A broken or dirty switch keeps it stopped even with the lid shut.
  3. The belt is worn or brokenOn machines with a belt, a loose or snapped belt means the motor runs but the drum doesn't. You'll often hear the motor humming.
  4. The capacitor is weakA failing capacitor gives the motor too little starting push. The spinner may turn slowly by hand but won't start on its own.
  5. The timer is faultyIf the spin timer doesn't click through, it never sends power to the motor. This is common on older machines.
  6. The spin motor is burntA burning smell, a tripping breaker or no sound at all can mean a burnt motor. It can usually be rewound instead of replaced.

The first point you can fix yourself. The rest need the machine opened up, so it's safer to let a repairman handle them.
