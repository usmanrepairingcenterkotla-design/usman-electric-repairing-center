Common questions

When should I service my fans?

In February or March, before summer. Repair work gets very busy from April.

Do you repair battery-operated DC fans?

Yes. We repair DC fans that run on batteries or solar, including inverter models.

Do you service fans for schools?

Yes. We service all the fans in a school or office in one visit. See the For Businesses page.
